## A township, not a city

{area.name} is a special-purpose district and census-designated place, mostly in Montgomery County with portions in Harris. It is run by The Woodlands Township and an elected board rather than by a city council, and residents voted against incorporation in 2021. Worth knowing before you go looking for a city permit office that does not exist.
