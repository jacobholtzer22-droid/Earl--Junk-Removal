## There is no Cypress city hall

{area.name} is an unincorporated community in Harris County, and it sits entirely inside the City of Houston extraterritorial jurisdiction. There is no separate city government, which is why questions about permits and rules get routed to the county or to Houston rather than answered locally. It changes nothing about a pickup. It changes a great deal about who you ring before a structure comes down.
