## Why the places are listed individually

Because three of them are not cities, and that trips people up. Cypress is an unincorporated community, Spring is a census-designated place, and The Woodlands is a township that voted against becoming a city. A postal address in any of them can sit outside the boundary a map shows you, which is why every page here says to give the address rather than the place name.

## If your address is not on the list
